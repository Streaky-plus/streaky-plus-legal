Users can initiate account deletion through the supported in-app process or request assistance through the public account-deletion process.

Online account deletion

Following successful completion, the deletion process removes the relevant online account and associated application-service information according to the implemented functionality.

Deleting the online account does not automatically erase all local habit records or other application data stored on the user's device.

Independently exported data

Copies previously exported or saved elsewhere are not automatically deleted when an online account is removed.

Subscriptions
