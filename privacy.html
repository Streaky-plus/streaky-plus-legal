Privacy Policy

How Streaky+ handles local information, optional accounts and cloud services.

Draft — verification required before app-store release.

These documents are being checked against the final app, cloud backend and publisher details. They are not final legal terms yet.

Draft reviewed: 13 November 2026 · Effective date: pending final review

1. Operator and contact

Streaky+ is a habit-tracking app. Contact the app operator at streakyplus.support@gmail.com. The final publisher identity must be confirmed before this policy becomes effective.

2. Data handled

  • Local: habits, completion history, streaks, notes, to-dos, reminders, preferences, achievements and weekly reviews are used on your device.
  • Accounts: when you choose to sign in, the service handles your email address, account identifier, login provider and authentication data.
  • Friends: names, friend codes, chosen avatars, connections, blocks and habit information you elect to share may be processed.
  • Optional cloud content: backups and optional profile pictures you choose to upload are stored remotely. Backups may include your habit and app content.
  • Payments: app-store and RevenueCat purchase identifiers and subscription/entitlement status are used to grant or restore purchases. Streaky+ does not collect full payment-card numbers through the app.
  • Technical data: providers may handle operational, diagnostic and security information needed to run the service; additional production telemetry must be verified before release.

3. Why data is used

To provide tracking features, authenticate accounts, enable chosen sharing, store or restore cloud content, manage purchases, respond to support requests and protect the service from misuse. Many local features can be used without an account.

4. Services and visibility

Streaky+ uses Supabase for accounts and selected cloud functions, RevenueCat and the applicable app store for purchase management, and Google where you use Google Sign-In or Google Play. Other providers must be checked against the final release build. Friends can see information according to the app's sharing settings; recipients may retain screenshots or copies of content shared with them.

5. Storage and security

Local information is stored on your device. Selected account, backup, photo and social information may be stored remotely. Network connections use HTTPS where provided. Cloud backups must not be assumed to be end-to-end encrypted.

6. Your choices

  • Use local features without an online account.
  • Adjust friend-sharing settings and remove optional profile photos.
  • Review or remove cloud backups in the app where supported.
  • Request account deletion using our deletion instructions.

7. Deletion and retention

The current app deletion flow removes the online account and associated Friends information, while local habits, notes and to-dos on that phone remain. The app presently asks users to delete cloud backups first; failures are reported rather than represented as success. Actual server cleanup and any legally required retention of purchase, security, or moderation records must be confirmed and disclosed before release.

8. Age and children's privacy

Age eligibility, the intended audience, and any additional safeguards require confirmation against the app's final Google Play audience settings and applicable law before publication.

9. Updates and questions

Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. For privacy questions or requests contact streakyplus.support@gmail.com.
