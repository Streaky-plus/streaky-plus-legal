1. Introduction

Welcome to Streaky+.

This Privacy Policy explains how Streaky+ ("we," "us," or "our") accesses, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our mobile application and associated online services.

Streaky+ is a habit-tracking and personal productivity application based on the open-source Streak project by InlitX.

We aim to give users meaningful control over their personal information while providing habit tracking, planning, statistics, optional cloud backup, account services, and social features.

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The Streaky+ Android application.
  • Account authentication and management.
  • Optional cloud backup and recovery.
  • Friends and optional progress sharing.
  • Purchase verification and premium entitlements.
  • Related support and account-deletion requests.

Please read this policy to understand how your information is handled.

2. Information We Process

The information processed by Streaky+ depends on the features you choose to use.

You can use supported offline functionality without creating an online account.

2.1 Information Stored Locally

Streaky+ stores application information on your device to provide its core functionality.

This may include:

  • Habit names, descriptions, and schedules.
  • Habit completion history and streaks.
  • Goals, tracking values, and progress records.
  • To-do lists and planning information.
  • Focus timer information.
  • Statistics and achievements.
  • Weekly Reviews and personal reflections.
  • Personal commitments and their outcomes.
  • App preferences, appearance settings, and selected backgrounds.
  • Other content you create through supported local features.

This information is primarily stored on your device.

Local information is not automatically uploaded to Streaky+ servers simply because you create a habit or complete a task.

However, some locally stored information can be included when you voluntarily use cloud backup.

2.2 Account Information

If you sign in, Streaky+ processes information required to create and manage your account.

Depending on the authentication method, this may include:

  • Email address.
  • Account identifier.
  • Display name.
  • Profile image or avatar.
  • Authentication session information.
  • Account creation and update information.

The audited application provides Google sign-in through Supabase Authentication.

When using Google sign-in, Google processes authentication information according to its own privacy practices.

Streaky+ receives the information necessary to authenticate you and provide supported account features.

Your Google password is not collected by Streaky+ through the Google sign-in process.

2.3 Profile and Friends Information

If you use Friends functionality, Streaky+ may process:

  • Your display name and selected avatar.
  • Your friend code.
  • Friend requests and accepted connections.
  • Friend removal and blocking information.
  • Per-friend sharing preferences.
  • Supported progress summaries.
  • Unlocked achievements or badges.
  • Other profile indicators displayed by the feature, such as purchase-status indicators where applicable.

Basic profile information may be discoverable through the supported friend-code lookup process.

Additional progress information is controlled through the application's available friend-sharing permissions.

We do not automatically grant friends access to your detailed private habit records or personal reflections.

2.4 Cloud Backup Information

If you choose to use cloud backup, Streaky+ creates a backup archive containing supported application data.

Depending on the data present and supported backup format, the archive may contain:

  • Habit records and progress.
  • Tasks and planning information.
  • Application preferences.
  • Statistics and achievements.
  • Saved Weekly Reviews and reflections.
  • Other supported local application records and associated content.

The archive is uploaded to the cloud storage service associated with your account.

The current backup process involves choosing whether to upload the backup archive; it does not necessarily provide individual record-by-record selection.

Cloud backups may therefore contain information that normally remains local when cloud backup is not used.

Cloud backup is optional and distinct from automatic multi-device synchronization.

Streaky+ does not currently provide automatic multi-device habit synchronization.

2.5 Purchases and Subscription Information

Streaky+ offers optional Lifetime purchases and Plus subscriptions.

Purchases are processed through Google Play, with RevenueCat used to support purchase verification and entitlement management.

Purchase-related information may include:

  • Purchase or subscription identifiers.
  • Purchased product identifiers.
  • Subscription status and expiration information.
  • Purchase tokens or transaction records.
  • Application user or purchase-management identifiers.
  • Device and technical information used for purchase verification.
  • Information needed to restore qualifying purchases.

Streaky+ does not directly collect payment-card details through its in-app Google Play purchase flow.

Payment details are handled by Google Play and its applicable payment-processing services.

2.6 Technical and Service Information

When you use online features, the relevant service providers may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

Depending on the provider and feature, this may include:

  • Device type and operating system.
  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Application identifiers.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Connection and authentication metadata.
  • Purchase-verification information.
  • Service error or diagnostic information.

This information may be processed by Supabase, RevenueCat, Google, or their infrastructure providers according to the services involved.

3. How We Use Information

We process information for the following purposes:

Application functionality

To provide habit tracking, schedules, progress calculations, statistics, planning tools, reminders, and other supported features.

Account management

To authenticate users, maintain account sessions, support account-related functionality, and protect against unauthorized access.

Cloud backup

To upload, store, retrieve, and delete backup archives when users choose to use supported cloud services.

Friends

To manage friend connections, enforce sharing preferences, display authorized information, and support removal or blocking.

Purchase management

To verify purchases, restore eligible entitlements, manage Lifetime and Plus access, and prevent unauthorized purchase access.

Security and reliability

To help maintain service availability, investigate technical problems, protect user accounts, and prevent abuse of online features.

Customer support

To respond to support requests, account problems, purchase questions, privacy inquiries, and deletion requests.

Legal obligations

To meet applicable legal requirements and respond to lawful requests where necessary.

Where applicable privacy laws require a legal basis for processing, the relevant basis may include providing requested services, complying with legal obligations, legitimate security and operational interests, or consent for optional activities.

4. Local Storage and Weekly Reviews

Streaky+ is designed to support substantial offline functionality.

Your habit information, tasks, statistics, preferences, and Weekly Review records are primarily managed on your device.

Weekly Reviews can contain:

  • Habit completion statistics.
  • Comparisons with previous weeks.
  • Best-performing or challenging habits.
  • Personal reflections.
  • Selected habit adjustments.
  • Personal commitments and outcomes.

These records are not automatically published to your friends.

Important: If you voluntarily create and upload a cloud backup, supported Weekly Review records may be included in that archive.

Deleting your online account does not automatically erase locally stored records on your device.

5. Cloud Backup and Storage

Streaky+ uses Supabase-related infrastructure for supported account and cloud functionality.

When you use cloud backup, your application data is transmitted to the configured cloud storage service.

Security

Supported cloud operations use encrypted network connections, such as HTTPS/TLS.

Access to account-associated cloud information is managed through authentication and relevant access controls.

Backups may use compression, integrity validation, and additional reliability checks.

However, these measures are not the same as end-to-end encryption.

Streaky+ does not currently claim to provide end-to-end encryption for cloud backups.

Users should understand that data uploaded to a cloud service is processed by the relevant infrastructure provider.

No online storage system can guarantee absolute security or availability.

User control

Users can manage supported cloud backups through the application's available data-management functionality.

Cloud backups must be removed as part of the current account-deletion workflow.

Streaky+ also attempts server-side cleanup of associated files during account deletion.

6. Friends and Privacy Controls

Friends functionality is optional.

Streaky+ uses sharing controls intended to limit which information other users can access.

Profile visibility

Certain profile details may be available through the supported friend-discovery mechanism, such as a friend-code lookup.

Accepted friends

Accepted friends may receive access to information authorized through your sharing preferences.

This can include supported progress summaries and unlocked badges.

Private information

Friends do not automatically receive access to:

  • Your detailed private habit logs.
  • Personal reflections.
  • Complete Weekly Review records.
  • Private tasks and notes.
  • Cloud backup archives.
  • Account authentication credentials.

Changing permissions

Users can modify available sharing permissions, remove friends, or block users.

These changes affect access through Streaky+'s supported systems.

However, content another person has already copied, recorded, or captured outside the application may remain outside our control.

Users should only share information they are comfortable making available to the selected recipients.

8. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Streaky+ processes and discloses information only as relevant to the features, services, and circumstances described in this Policy.

Information may be disclosed:

To service providers: When required to provide authentication, cloud storage, and purchase verification.

To selected friends: According to the supported friend-discovery process and sharing permissions.

At your direction: When you voluntarily upload content, share information, or export data.

For legal or security reasons: When required by applicable law or reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, unauthorized access, or serious service abuse.

We do not treat another user's access to your information as permission to access unrelated private records.

9. Data Retention

How long information is retained depends on its type and how it is used.

Local application data

Locally stored information generally remains on your device until you delete it through supported controls, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application.

Deleting an online account does not necessarily remove local application data.

Account information

Account-associated information is retained while necessary to operate the account and is subject to the applicable deletion process when the account is deleted.

Cloud backups

Uploaded backups remain associated with cloud storage until removed through supported deletion functionality or the applicable service cleanup process.

Friends information

Friend relationships, sharing permissions, and associated online records are managed through the relevant account and friend-deletion operations.

Purchase records

Google Play and RevenueCat may retain purchase-related information according to their applicable policies, operational requirements, and legal obligations.

Deleting a Streaky+ account does not necessarily erase records independently maintained by Google Play.

Operational records

Some service providers may retain limited operational, security, or backup records for additional periods according to their infrastructure and legal requirements.

We do not promise that every record held by an independent third-party provider is immediately erased following an application-level deletion request.

Where applicable, users may contact us to request more information about retention and deletion.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Streaky+ provides an account-deletion process.

Deleting cloud backups

The current application instructs users to remove their cloud backups before completing online account deletion.

The deletion service also attempts to remove remaining associated backup and profile-photo files.

Deleting the online account

Once the account-deletion process completes successfully, the application removes the associated online account and covered service records according to the implemented deletion workflow, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Local information remains separate

Deleting your online Streaky+ account does not automatically erase locally stored habits, tasks, schedules, or other records on your device.

To remove local records, users may use supported data-management options or Android's application-storage controls.

Clearing app storage may permanently remove local records that have not been backed up.

Independent copies

Files exported to external storage or independently saved elsewhere are not automatically removed through online account deletion.

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting a Streaky+ account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription.

Subscriptions must be managed separately through Google Play.

Account deletion without app access

Users who cannot access the application may request assistance using the public account-deletion webpage or by contacting:

streakyplus.support@gmail.com

Account-deletion webpage: https://hussain-maxweb.github.io/streaky-plus-legal/delete-account.html

11. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through Streaky+.

These include, where implemented and applicable:

  • Authentication for account-based services.
  • Encrypted network connections.
  • Account-associated cloud access controls.
  • Restrictions on friend-specific information access.
  • Backup integrity validation.
  • Purchase entitlement verification.

Despite these measures, no software application, storage system, or internet service is completely immune to security risks.

Users are encouraged to keep their devices updated, protect account access, and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.
