1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Streaky+ ("we," "us," or "our") accesses, collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when users access the mobile application and its associated online services.

Streaky+ is a habit-tracking and personal productivity application based on the open-source Streak project by InlitX.

Some functionality is available offline, while certain optional services require an online account or internet access.

This Policy applies to the Streaky+ Android application and associated account, cloud, social and purchase-management services.

2. Information We Process

The information processed depends on the features users choose.

2.1 Locally Stored Information

Streaky+ stores application information on a user's device to provide supported functionality.

This may include:

  • Habit names and descriptions.
  • Schedules and reminders.
  • Completion records and streaks.
  • Habit tracking values.
  • Tasks, notes and planning information.
  • Focus-session information.
  • Statistics and achievements.
  • Weekly Reviews.
  • Personal reflections and commitments.
  • Appearance preferences.
  • Custom backgrounds and selected images.
  • Other supported application records.

Creating or completing a habit does not, by itself, mean that the record is automatically transmitted to Streaky+'s cloud storage.

However, supported local records may be included if the user chooses to upload a cloud backup.

2.2 Account Information

Users who sign in may provide or authorize access to:

  • Email address.
  • Account identifier.
  • Display name.
  • Avatar or profile image.
  • Authentication session information.
  • Account creation or update metadata.

The audited application version supports Google sign-in through Supabase Authentication.

Google handles the authentication process under its own policies.

Streaky+ receives the information necessary to authenticate the user and operate the relevant account features.

Streaky+ does not collect the user's Google password through Google sign-in.

2.3 Friends and Profile Information

Using the Friends feature may involve processing:

  • Public-facing display name.
  • Selected avatar.
  • Friend code.
  • Friend connections.
  • Friend removal and blocking records.
  • Friend-specific sharing preferences.
  • Authorized progress summaries.
  • Unlocked badges.
  • Supported account or profile indicators displayed in the Friends interface.

Basic profile information may be visible during supported friend-code lookup.

Additional information is shared according to the applicable friendship and sharing permissions.

Friends do not automatically receive access to private detailed habit logs, personal reflections or cloud backup archives.

2.4 Cloud Backup Information

If a user chooses cloud backup, Streaky+ may upload an archive containing supported application data.

Depending on the records present and the backup format, that archive may include:

  • Habits and completion history.
  • Tasks and planning records.
  • Application settings.
  • Statistics and achievements.
  • Weekly Reviews and reflections.
  • Other supported local data and associated content.

Users choose whether to upload the backup archive.

The current backup functionality does not necessarily allow users to select each individual record included in the archive.

Cloud backup is optional and is not the same as automatic multi-device synchronization.

Automatic multi-device habit synchronization is not currently implemented in Streaky+.

2.5 Purchases and Subscription Information

Streaky+ offers optional purchases through Google Play.

RevenueCat supports purchase verification and entitlement management.

Purchase-related information may include:

  • Product identifiers.
  • Transaction identifiers.
  • Subscription status.
  • Subscription expiration information.
  • Purchase-verification records or tokens.
  • Purchase-management user identifiers.
  • Information needed to restore valid purchases.
  • Relevant technical information used by the purchase services.

Payment-card information used in Google Play checkout is processed by Google Play and its payment services, not directly collected through a Streaky+ payment form.

2.6 Technical Information

Online providers may process technical information necessary to operate and secure their services.

Depending on the provider, this may include:

  • Device and operating-system information.
  • Application identifiers.
  • IP addresses.
  • Authentication and request metadata.
  • Network timestamps.
  • Purchase-verification information.
  • Service diagnostics and error information.

The exact information depends on the relevant service and its configuration.

3. Why Information Is Processed

Streaky+ and its service providers process information for purposes including:

Core functionality: Operating habits, tasks, reminders, statistics, planning and related features.

Authentication: Creating and maintaining supported account sessions.

Cloud backup: Uploading, retrieving, managing and deleting user-requested backup archives.

Friends: Managing connections, sharing preferences and authorized profile or progress information.

Purchases: Verifying and restoring Lifetime and Plus entitlements.

Security: Protecting accounts, preventing abuse and investigating technical problems.

Support: Responding to account, purchase, privacy and deletion requests.

Legal compliance: Meeting applicable legal obligations.

Where required by applicable privacy law, processing relies on an appropriate legal basis, which may include providing requested services, consent, legal obligations or legitimate security and operational interests.

4. Local Storage and Weekly Reviews

Streaky+ supports significant offline functionality.

Habit records, tasks, statistics, preferences and Weekly Review information are primarily managed locally.

Weekly Reviews may contain:

  • Habit completion calculations.
  • Comparisons with previous weeks.
  • Strongest and most challenging habits.
  • Personal reflections.
  • Habit adjustments.
  • Personal commitments and their outcomes.

Weekly Reviews are not automatically published to friends.

However, saved Weekly Review information may be included in an optional uploaded cloud backup.

Local operation does not mean that optional backup archives exclude Weekly Review data.

5. Cloud Storage and Security

Supported cloud backup functionality uses Supabase-related infrastructure.

When a backup is uploaded, the archive is transmitted to the configured cloud storage service.

Cloud operations use security measures such as encrypted network connections and account-associated access controls.

Backup archives may also use compression and integrity validation.

These protections are not equivalent to end-to-end encryption.

Streaky+ does not currently claim that cloud backups are end-to-end encrypted.

Cloud infrastructure providers may process information necessary to store, secure and operate the service.

No online system can guarantee absolute protection against every security threat or service interruption.

Users can manage supported backups through the available application controls.

6. Friends and Sharing Controls

Friends functionality is optional.

Certain basic profile details may be accessible through the supported friend-discovery process.

Accepted friends may view information authorized through the application's available sharing controls.

This can include supported progress summaries and unlocked achievements.

Users can manage supported sharing permissions and friend relationships.

Private habit logs, reflections, tasks and cloud backup archives are not automatically made available to friends.

Changing or revoking sharing permissions restricts future access through Streaky+'s systems according to the implemented controls.

Information previously copied, captured or saved outside the application may remain beyond our control.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be processed or disclosed:

  • To providers necessary for supported account, cloud or purchase functionality.
  • To selected friends according to authorized sharing preferences.
  • At the user's direction, including backup uploads and data exports.
  • In response to lawful requests or applicable legal obligations.
  • When reasonably necessary to investigate serious fraud, unauthorized access or security abuse.

Sharing a specific progress summary does not authorize access to unrelated private information.

9. Data Retention

Retention depends on the information involved, the features used and applicable provider requirements.

9.1 Local Information

Local records generally remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears application storage or uninstalls the application.

Deleting an online account does not necessarily remove locally stored information.

9.2 Account Information

Account-associated information is maintained as necessary to provide the account and associated services.

It is subject to the applicable account-deletion process and any legally required retention.

9.3 Cloud Backups

Uploaded cloud backups remain stored until deleted through supported controls or relevant account-deletion cleanup.

9.4 Friends Records

Friend connections and relevant online sharing information are managed through account and friendship controls and associated deletion operations.

9.5 Purchase Information

Google Play and RevenueCat may retain purchase information independently according to their applicable policies and legal obligations.

Deleting a Streaky+ account does not necessarily delete Google's independent transaction records.

9.6 Operational and Security Records

Service providers may retain limited operational, backup or security information according to their infrastructure configuration and applicable obligations.

Streaky+ does not guarantee that every independent third-party record is erased immediately upon account deletion.

Users may contact support to request further information about applicable retention practices.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Streaky+ provides a process for deleting online accounts.

Cloud backups

Users are instructed to delete existing cloud backups before completing account deletion.

The server-side deletion service also attempts to remove remaining associated backup and profile-photo files.

Online account information

Successful account deletion removes the relevant online account and covered service information according to the implemented workflow, subject to applicable legal retention.

Local data

Deleting the online account does not automatically erase local habits, tasks, schedules or other device-stored records.

Users can separately delete supported records or clear the application's local storage.

Clearing local storage can permanently erase information that has not been backed up.

Exported files

Independent exported copies or information saved in other locations are not automatically removed by online account deletion.

Purchase records

Google Play and RevenueCat may maintain independent records necessary for billing, purchase management or applicable obligations.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription.

External deletion requests

Users unable to access the application may follow the public account-deletion instructions or contact support.

11. Information Security

Streaky+ uses security measures appropriate to its supported functions, including where implemented:

  • Account authentication.
  • Encrypted network communication.
  • Account-associated cloud permissions.
  • Friend-specific sharing controls.
  • Backup integrity validation.
  • Purchase entitlement verification.

These controls reduce certain risks but cannot guarantee complete security.

Users should keep their devices and account access protected.

  • Use available offline functionality without an account.
  • Review or update supported account details.
  • Choose whether to upload cloud backups.
  • Delete supported cloud backups.
  • Manage Friends permissions.
  • Remove or block friends.
  • Delete local application records.
  • Request online account deletion.
  • Request access, correction or deletion of applicable personal information.
  • Contact support regarding other available privacy rights.

Some requests may require identity verification to prevent unauthorized access to another person's information.

Requests are handled subject to applicable law and relevant exceptions.
