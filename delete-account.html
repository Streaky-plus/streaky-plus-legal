Account & data removal

Delete your account

How to request deletion of your Streaky+ online account, including without the app.

Option A · Delete from the app

  1. Open Streaky+ and sign in to the account you want deleted.
  2. Go to Account → Delete account.
  3. Remove stored cloud backups using Data → Cloud Backup first if prompted.
  4. Read the warning, confirm, and reauthenticate if required.

If an error occurs, do not assume deletion succeeded; contact support.

Option B · Request without the app

Email streakyplus.support@gmail.com with subject Streaky+ account deletion request from the account email. Ask for deletion of your online account and associated cloud data. If you used Google Sign-In, use its associated account email. Do not send passwords, verification codes or other secrets.

The support team must securely verify that you own the account before acting. The request-handling and backend cleanup process require final operator testing before public release.

What gets deleted?

The current in-app workflow is intended to remove the online account and its related Friends data. Cloud backups currently must be removed first. The full backend deletion scope and any retention exceptions must be verified before release.

What stays on your device?

Current app wording says habits, notes and to-dos stored locally on the phone remain after online account deletion. You can remove local app data separately using the app or device settings.

Subscriptions

Deleting your Streaky+ online account does not cancel a Google Play Plus subscription. Cancel separately at Google Play subscription management.

Data retained where required

Some payment, security or compliance records may need limited lawful retention. Categories and timeframes must be finalized in the published Privacy Policy.
